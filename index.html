Why Outwoods?

We are not trying to be everything to everyone. We are a friendly competitive club that wants enough good, reliable players to put two strong sides out every Saturday.

01

Regular cricket

Two Saturday league teams means genuine opportunities across different standards — not just being another name on a huge squad list.

02

Friendly club

Competitive on the pitch, relaxed off it. New players, students and people moving into Loughborough are very welcome.

03

Affordable

Annual playing membership is £45. Come to a net first, meet the group, and see whether it feels right.

University?

Want regular Saturday cricket alongside uni?

You do not have to choose between university cricket and Outwoods. If you want additional competitive Saturday cricket — whether you are an undergraduate, postgrad, PhD student or member of staff — get in touch.

  • Regular 1st & 2nd XI opportunities
  • Great fit alongside university cricket
  • Particularly useful for summer availability
