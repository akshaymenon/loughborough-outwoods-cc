About the club

Loughborough Outwoods Cricket Club is a friendly, inclusive club based in Loughborough. We field competitive senior teams and welcome players of all experience levels.

01

Saturday cricket

Our two senior teams play league cricket on Saturdays throughout the summer.

02

Everyone welcome

Whether you have played for years or are looking for a new club, come and say hello.

03

More than cricket

We enjoy our cricket, support our teammates and keep the social side of the club alive too.
