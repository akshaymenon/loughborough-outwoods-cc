About Outwoods

Who we are.

Loughborough Outwoods Cricket Club is a friendly, inclusive club based in Loughborough. We field competitive senior teams, run junior cricket and welcome people into the game at different ages and experience levels. Our focus is simple: enjoy our cricket, support our teammates, and grow the game in our community.
