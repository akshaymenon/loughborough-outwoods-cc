The club

Cricket, mates and a good Saturday.

Loughborough Outwoods Cricket Club is a friendly, inclusive club based in Loughborough. We field competitive senior teams and welcome players of all experience levels.

Our focus is simple: enjoy our cricket, support our teammates, and grow the game in our community.

More about Outwoods

Our two senior teams play league cricket throughout the summer.
