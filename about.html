About LOCC

Our club.

Loughborough Outwoods Cricket Club is a friendly, inclusive club based in Loughborough. We field competitive senior teams and welcome players of all experience levels. Our focus is simple: enjoy our cricket, support our teammates, and grow the game in our community.

Cricket at Outwoods

We have two senior Saturday teams playing in the Leicestershire & Rutland Cricket League.

We train through the winter, play throughout the summer and welcome new players looking to get involved with the club.

Part of Loughborough

Outwoods is a local club and we are proud to be part of the cricket community in and around Loughborough.

Players, supporters, volunteers and local businesses all play a part in keeping the club going.
