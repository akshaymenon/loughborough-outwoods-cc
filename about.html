About LOCC

Local club.
Proper cricket.

Loughborough Outwoods is a volunteer-run cricket club based in Loughborough, Leicestershire. We want to be competitive without taking ourselves too seriously — a club people enjoy playing for and stick around at.

What we're about

Saturday league cricket is the centre of the senior club. We run a 1st XI and 2nd XI, organise winter nets, and keep the social side of the club alive too.

  • Welcoming to new players
  • Competitive but friendly
  • Volunteer-run
  • Affordable membership
  • Part of the local Loughborough cricket community

Who fits here?

Current players, people moving to the area, students who want more cricket, older juniors stepping into adult cricket, and anyone looking for a club where they can genuinely contribute.

Good cricket. Good people. Less Friday-night panic.

Club life, not stock photography.
