Sponsorship

Why sponsor us?

Sponsorship helps us cover key club costs, invest in equipment and support the club's growth in the local community.

  1. Help fund our scoreboard, sight screens and other key equipment.
  2. Support annual costs including cricket balls and umpires.
  3. Help us grow our membership and community engagement.
