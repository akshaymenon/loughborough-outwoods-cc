Sponsorship

Why sponsor us?

Sponsorship offers critical financial support, allowing us to cover expenses and support our growth initiatives. It also helps strengthen the links between the club, local businesses and the wider community.

By sponsoring us, your business can build brand recognition and connect with our players, supporters and wider network.

Through sponsorship you will support the following objectives:

  1. Help fund our new scoreboard, new sight screens and other key equipment.
  2. Help with some of our larger annual costs, including cricket balls and umpires.
  3. Support the expansion of our membership and help develop our social and local community engagement.
