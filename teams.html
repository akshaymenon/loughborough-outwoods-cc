The cricket

Two XIs.
Every Saturday.

Our senior focus is simple: field a competitive 1st XI and 2nd XI every Saturday, with enough depth that availability does not turn Friday night into a recruitment emergency.

1st XI

Competitive cricket. Clear opportunities.

The 1st XI selects first and represents the top end of the senior club. We are particularly keen to add quality bowling and all-round depth for 2027.

  • Bowlers and all-rounders are the main priority
  • Batsmen welcome where they strengthen the side
  • Regular Saturday availability matters
2nd XI

Dependable depth matters.

The 2nd XI is where availability shortages hit hardest. We want a proper, reliable squad — not a side rebuilt from scratch every Friday.

  • Dependable bowlers / all-rounders are a major need
  • Reliable general players are valuable
  • Good fit for players wanting meaningful Saturday cricket

What we're building for 2027

Registration numbers alone are not the goal. We care about usable Saturday depth.

Adult playing members40headline target
Active players30+meaningful season involvement
Core Saturday group24–26regular squad depth

Selection

The 1st XI captain selects first. The 2nd XI captain then selects from the remaining available players. That is why depth and reliability matter so much — especially for the 2s.

More cricket

There is no planned 3rd XI. If we have surplus availability, we may occasionally organise Sunday friendlies — but the priority is making two strong Saturday sides sustainable.
