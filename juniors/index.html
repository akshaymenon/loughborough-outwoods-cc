Why we run junior cricket

More than a coaching session.

We started junior cricket at Outwoods to give local children a friendly first experience of the game and to help cricket grow in the Loughborough community.

For us, that means more than learning to bat, bowl and field. We want Friday evenings at the Woods to be somewhere children enjoy coming back to, parents feel part of the club, friendships grow and families can spend time together around the game.

Over time, we also want to create a genuine pathway for young players who love cricket to keep developing and, when they are ready, progress towards senior cricket at Outwoods.

Our junior cricket

Fun first. Skills follow.

Junior cricket at Outwoods is mixed and designed to be welcoming for children who are new to the game as well as those who already love cricket.

Dynamos Cricket

Our main junior programme. Dynamos is designed for children aged 8–11, with fun, game-based sessions that build batting, bowling and fielding skills. Dynamos will return to Outwoods in summer 2027.

All Stars

We are exploring adding All Stars Cricket for younger children aged 5–8 in 2027, giving families an earlier first step into cricket at Outwoods.

Winter cricket

We are also considering indoor junior sessions over winter. Families interested in winter cricket can contact Liam to register their interest.

Summer at the Woods

Friday evenings at Nanpantan.

Our 2026 Dynamos programme ran every Friday evening at Nanpantan Sports Ground on Watermead Lane, Loughborough.

The aim was simple: give children plenty of cricket, keep the sessions enjoyable and create a relaxed club atmosphere that families want to be part of.

We will be running junior cricket again in summer 2027. Exact dates, times and registration details will be published once the programme is confirmed.

Who can join?

New to cricket? Absolutely fine.

Our junior sessions are mixed and open to boys and girls. Children do not need previous club-cricket experience to get involved.

Dynamos is aimed at 8–11-year-olds. If All Stars is added for 2027, that programme will provide an option for 5–8-year-olds too.

If you are unsure which programme would suit your child, contact Liam and we can point you in the right direction.

A pathway into the club

From first ball to senior cricket.

We want young players to see Outwoods as a club they can grow with, not somewhere they attend for a few summer sessions and then disappear from.

As our junior programme develops, our aim is to help interested players keep progressing, build confidence and stay connected to cricket, with a route towards senior club cricket when they reach the right age and stage.

Community

A club for the whole family.

Outwoods is a volunteer-led local cricket club. We want junior evenings to feel like part of club life: children playing, parents chatting, families spending time at the ground and new people feeling welcome.

That community matters just as much to us as developing the next generation of cricketers.
