Come have a net.

You do not need to know anyone at the club. Tell us a little about your cricket and availability, then we will get you involved in a net or chat.

What we're looking for

  • Players who want regular Saturday cricket
  • 1st XI-quality bowlers and all-rounders
  • Dependable 2nd XI bowlers and all-rounders
  • Reliable general players and batsmen
  • Students, postgrads, PhDs and university staff welcome

Wicketkeepers are welcome too — we just have less urgency there because we already have cover.

What happens next?

Simple. We make contact, get you to a suitable net, and work out whether Outwoods is a good fit for you.

Can I play alongside Loughborough University cricket?

Yes. That is exactly how we want to position the club: regular Saturday league cricket alongside university cricket, not instead of it.

Do I need to be a strong player?

No. We have two Saturday XIs and need players across different levels. We will be straightforward about where we think you fit.

How much is membership?

The current annual playing membership is £45.

Do you run a 3rd XI?

No planned 3rd XI. Our focus is having enough depth to put out strong 1st and 2nd XIs every Saturday. Sunday friendlies may occasionally be organised when availability allows.
