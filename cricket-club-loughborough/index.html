Saturday league cricket at Outwoods

Loughborough Outwoods Cricket Club is based at Nanpantan Sports Ground on Watermead Lane, Loughborough. We field a 1st XI and 2nd XI every Saturday during the summer in the Leicestershire & Rutland Cricket League.

We are a welcoming, family-friendly club for experienced league cricketers, players returning to the game and people who have recently moved to Loughborough and want regular competitive cricket.

Why Outwoods?

Regular cricket. Friendly club.

Two Saturday XIs

Competitive league cricket across our 1st XI and 2nd XI, giving players opportunities at different standards.

Training & winter nets

Winter net sessions at Loughborough's ECB cricket facilities, plus summer training during the season.

More than cricket

A welcoming local club with socials, BBQs and a strong community around the teams.

New to Loughborough?

Regular Saturday cricket alongside university or work

If you are studying or working at Loughborough University, Outwoods can give you regular Saturday league cricket alongside your university commitments. We are particularly keen to hear from students, postgraduates, PhD students and university staff who want consistent weekend cricket through the summer.

You do not need to choose between university cricket and Outwoods. If you want more competitive Saturday cricket, come and speak to us.

New players wanted

All abilities welcome

We welcome players across a range of standards. For the 2027 season we are especially interested in bowlers and all-rounders who expect to be available regularly on Saturdays, but anyone looking for a cricket club in Loughborough is welcome to get in touch.

Where we play

Nanpantan Sports Ground

Our home ground is Nanpantan Sports Ground, Watermead Lane, Loughborough, Leicestershire, LE11 3TN.

It is a local Loughborough ground and the base for our Saturday league teams throughout the summer.

Common questions

Playing cricket in Loughborough

When do you play?

Our senior teams play league cricket on Saturdays through the summer, with occasional Sunday friendlies when availability allows.

Can students join?

Yes. Loughborough University students, postgraduates and staff are welcome, including players who also play university cricket.

What standard is the cricket?

We run both a 1st XI and 2nd XI, so there are opportunities across different standards of competitive club cricket.
